Executive Summary

Democratizing Hardware Innovation with Zero Risk.

Hardware innovation is currently gatekept by high costs, fear of failure, and supply chain complexity. Robo Sparrow solves this by creating the "Figma for Robotics" — a browser-based simulation engine where users design, code, and test IoT devices virtually before buying a single component.

The "Unicorn" Insight

By combining an Education Platform, a Simulation Tool, and a Component Marketplace, Robo Sparrow captures value at every stage of the hardware lifecycle: Learning, Prototyping, and Procurement.

The "Flywheel" Business Model

Unlike pure EdTech (low retention) or pure E-commerce (low margin), Robo Sparrow creates a self-reinforcing ecosystem. Users learn for free, creating content that attracts more users, who then buy hardware, generating revenue to improve the platform.

The AI Advantage

Agent-Assisted Design

The AI doesn't just chat; it wires. Users describe a goal ("Make a plant watering system"), and the Agent selects components, wires them in the sim, and writes the C++/MicroPython code.

Instant Component Sim

Using generative models, we approximate the behavior of new sensors/chips instantly, allowing users to test parts that aren't manually modeled yet.
