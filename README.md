# Robo Sparrow 🐦🤖 - AI Powered Virtual Robotics Studio
**"Build/Learn First, Buy Later."**

> *Remember the time you bought a $200 robot kit, plugged in one wrong wire, and watched magic smoke escape from your brand new microcontroller? Yeah, we've been there too. That's why we built Robo Sparrow.*

---

## 🌍 The Vision
We believe robotics education should be accessible to **everyone**, regardless of their budget. Learning to build robots shouldn't require expensive hardware kits that gather dust after one use. 

**Robo Sparrow** is a Virtual Robotics Studio that lets you design, code, and simulate robots right in your browser. Our goal is simple: **Democratize Hardware Learning.**

Whether you're a student in a dorm room or a hobbyist on a budget, you can start building *now*. No shipping times, no burnt components, just pure innovation.

## 🚀 Current Features (Early Access)
- **Virtual Workbench**: Drag-and-drop components like Arduino Uno, LEDs, Motors, and Sensors.
- **Real-Time Wiring**: Smart wire routing that respects polarity and logic.
- **Hybrid Code Engine**: 
    - Write standard **C++ Arduino code**.
    - **Instant Simulation**: Run your code immediately in the browser.
    - **AI Assistant**: Stuck? Our Gemini-powered AI helps you write, debug, and optimize your code.
- **Physics-Lite Simulation**: Watch your 2WD or 4WD robot drive around a virtual environment based on your actual code and wiring!

## 🔮 The Roadmap
We are just getting started. Here is where we are heading:

### 🛠️ Advanced Simulation Core
- **Custom Chassis Design**: Design your own robot body frame to fit your specific needs.
- **Environment Builder**: Test your robot in mazes, line-following tracks, and obstacle courses.

### 🤖 Generative AI Components
- **"If you can dream it, you can simulate it."**
- Can't find a specific sensor? Ask our AI to generate a **Lite Version** of the component instantly so you don't have to wait. and in the mean time we will be working on it behind the scenes to make that component available for you.
- **Community Library**: Users can upload their own custom simulated components. And get certain rewards. Earn as you build and share.

### 🛒 The "Smart" Marketplace
- **Seamless Integration**: Once your virtual robot works perfectly, we'll tell you exactly what parts you need to build it in real life.
- **Best Price Guarantee**: We will partner with manufacturers to offer the specific components you need at the best prices.
- **Verified Compatibility**: No more guessing. If it works in Robo Sparrow, the kit we send you will work in reality.

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS
- **AI Engine**: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash / Pro
- **Simulation**: Custom JavaScript Physics Engine
