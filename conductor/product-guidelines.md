# Product Guidelines for Robo Sparrow

## Prose Style
The prose style for all user-facing documentation and marketing materials should be **technical but also easy to understand**. The target audience includes both advanced users and novices who are just starting to learn coding and robotics. Therefore, clarity, conciseness, and a direct approach are paramount, while also ensuring the content is accessible and not overly complex for beginners.

## Messaging
The core message that Robo Sparrow should consistently convey across all communications is: **"You can learn anything related to hardware here without needing to buy physical kits, but you also have the option to purchase them from us if you wish."** This message highlights the value proposition of virtual learning and the seamless transition to physical hardware provided by Robo Sparrow. It reinforces the "Build/Learn First, Buy Later" vision, offering a risk-free learning environment with a clear path to real-world application.
